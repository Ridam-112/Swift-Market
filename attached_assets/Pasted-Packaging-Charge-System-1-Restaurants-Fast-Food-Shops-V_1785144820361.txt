Packaging Charge System

1. Restaurants & Fast Food Shops
--------------------------------
- Vendors can set their own custom packaging charges.
- The packaging charge will be applied per order.
- Example:
    - Restaurant A -> Rs. 10
    - Restaurant B -> Rs. 25
    - Restaurant C -> Rs. 15

The vendor can change this value anytime from their vendor panel.

2. All Other Shop Categories
----------------------------
- Vendors cannot edit packaging charges.
- Packaging charges will be managed only by the SwiftMart Admin Panel.

Examples:
- Grocery -> Rs. 6
- Medicine -> Rs. 6
- Bakery -> Rs. 8
- Stationery -> Rs. 5
- Cosmetics -> Rs. 6
- Electronics -> Rs. 10

The admin can configure packaging charges category-wise from the Admin Panel.

------------------------------------------------------------

GST System

- GST is optional and vendor specific.
- Only GST registered vendors should enable GST.

Vendor Panel:
- GST Applicable (Yes / No)
- GST Rate (Custom value)

Examples:
- 5%
- 12%
- 18%

GST Formula:

GST Amount = (Product Subtotal × GST Rate) / 100

GST should NOT be applied on:
- Delivery Charges
- Packaging Charges
- Platform Charges

------------------------------------------------------------

Checkout Flow

Subtotal of Products
+
Packaging Charge
+
GST Amount (if applicable)
+
Delivery Charge
=
Grand Total

------------------------------------------------------------

Vendor Permissions

Restaurants & Fast Food:
- Can set custom packaging charges.
- Can enable/disable GST.
- Can set GST rate.

All Other Vendors:
- Cannot edit packaging charges.
- Can enable/disable GST if applicable.
- Packaging charges are controlled by the Admin Panel.

------------------------------------------------------------

Admin Panel Requirements

Add category-wise packaging charge settings.

Examples:
- Grocery Packaging Charge
- Medicine Packaging Charge
- Bakery Packaging Charge
- Sweet Shop Packaging Charge
- Electronics Packaging Charge
- Clothing Packaging Charge
- Cosmetics Packaging Charge

Restaurants and Fast Food should be excluded from the admin-controlled packaging charge system because they manage their own packaging charges.

The implementation must be fully dynamic and scalable without any hardcoded values in the checkout logic.
